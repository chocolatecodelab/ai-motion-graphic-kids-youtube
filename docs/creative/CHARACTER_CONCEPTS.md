# Character Concepts (proposals for owner selection)

Status: concepts only. **No identity is approved.** All names need trademark and YouTube-search clearance before adoption. The concepts were written to avoid resemblance to existing kids' characters (no binocular-glasses scientist, no bird mascots, no famous explorer silhouettes).

Common to all concepts:
- Ages read as about 10–11 (a peer to the audience, slightly older than the youngest viewers).
- Humble: they don't know the answer and find it out alongside the viewer.
- Carries the **Discovery Compass**, a brass-and-teal pocket device whose needle points at "the answer". It opens portals, projects holograms, shrinks/zooms and time-jumps. The compass is also the recurring visual transition (portal wipe).
- Optional companion: **"Needle"**, a tiny holographic spark that lives in the compass. Needle is curious, literal-minded and asks the "silly" questions kids are shy to ask. Two voices make it a dialogue instead of a lecture.

---

## Concept A: "Juno Pathfinder"
- **Who:** an 11-year-old who grew up in a lighthouse and maps everything they find in a patchwork notebook.
- **Look:** big round eyes, freckles, a mop of curly hair under explorer goggles. **Explorer Blue** jacket with a **Discovery Yellow** scarf, **Adventure Orange** backpack with map tubes; compass on a cord.
- **Personality:** brave, enthusiastic, talks to objects, says "Hmm… wait a second!" when noticing a clue.
- **Catchphrases:** "Every question opens a door!" · "Compass, take us there!"
- **Strength:** classic adventure protagonist, strong silhouette (scarf + backpack). **Risk:** a human design needs inclusive, globally relatable choices.

## Concept B: "Ollie Ripple" (animal protagonist)
- **Who:** a young river otter explorer who left their riverbank to understand the whole world.
- **Look:** sleek rounded otter, oversized **Explorer Blue** satchel, **Discovery Yellow** neckerchief, compass worn like a wristwatch.
- **Personality:** playful, tumbles when excited, very tactile ("Let me *feel* how it works!").
- **Catchphrases:** "Let's dive into the why!" · "Ripple-tastic!"
- **Strength:** universally relatable, avoids ethnicity and cultural specificity, very expressive and cheap to rig, and a nature-pillar fit. **Risk:** less natural for history episodes; otters are a common mascot animal, so distinctiveness relies on design.

## Concept C: "Kai & Needle"
- **Who:** an inventive 10-year-old who *built* their Discovery Compass from parts found in a grandparent's attic. Needle is the compass's accidental AI spark.
- **Look:** short hair, oversized **Nature Teal** hoodie with rolled sleeves, utility belt with tools, **Adventure Orange** sneakers, a band-aid on one knee (a hint of hands-on tinkering).
- **Personality:** a maker; takes things apart to see how they work; gets things wrong and fixes them.
- **Catchphrases:** "Okay, Needle, show me the inside!" · "Mystery: solved… almost!"
- **Strength:** perfect fit for "How Things Work" plus science; the duo gives built-in dialogue. **Risk:** two characters means double the rig and voice work.

---

## Recommendation
**Concept C (Kai & Needle)**:
- best matches the priority pillars;
- the duo avoids lecture format;
- the invented compass justifies every magic transition.

**Concept B** is the low-cost alternative if a single, highly expressive animal hero is preferred. Final choice is the owner's.

## Next steps after selection (M2)
1. Produce 3–5 visual explorations per selected concept (turnaround, expression sheet) with image models. These are for *design exploration only* and are owner-reviewed.
2. Lock the final design and write the Character Bible v1, covering:
   - proportions (head ≈ 1/3.5 body height for a stylised kid read);
   - palette swatches;
   - silhouette test;
   - expression set: neutral, curious, surprised, delighted, puzzled, determined, worried-lite, laughing;
   - body-language rules;
   - movement (bouncy anticipation, quick settles);
   - voice brief;
   - do/don't sheet.
3. Build the rig per the chosen rig type and freeze `character_version = 1`.
