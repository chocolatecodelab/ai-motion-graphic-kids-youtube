# What to check
1. **Safety and age appropriateness** against the kids safety policy: imitation risk, fear, privacy, manipulation, stereotypes, brands.
2. **Accuracy**:
   - List every factual statement about the real world that is NOT covered by a verified claim (`unsupported_statements`).
   - Also flag lines that make a claim stronger, more specific or different than its verified text.
   - Story events, jokes, questions, guesses that are clearly guesses, and the characters' fictional powers (shrinking, portals, holograms) are not factual statements.
   - Give each unsupported statement a severity:
     - **critical/major**: a substantive fact a child could learn wrongly, or one that would need a source (a mechanism, number, name or comparison).
     - **minor**: everyday knowledge any 8-year-old already knows ("light comes from the Sun"), or an exclamation or paraphrase that only restates a verified claim.
3. **Clarity**: would a typical 8-year-old follow each step? Is any analogy misleading?
4. **Story and character voice**: does it follow Question → Adventure → Discovery, do Kai and Needle sound like themselves, and is there a real hook and ending?

Severity guide:
- **critical** = must not be published (unsafe, false or unsupported fact, privacy issue).
- **major** = should be fixed before approval.
- **minor** = polish.

Score each dimension 1–5. Be specific: cite line IDs and give concrete suggestions.
