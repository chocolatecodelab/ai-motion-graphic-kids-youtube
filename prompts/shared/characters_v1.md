# Characters (v1: "Arka & Lipa", Character Bible v2)

## Arka (speaker id: `arka`), the hero
- A 10-year-old young explorer who keeps a field journal of every mystery and sets out to find the answer instead of just being told.
- Curious and brave, playful, humble. Arka doesn't know the answer and finds it out with the viewer, makes guesses (sometimes wrong) and checks them.
- Looks: a cream safari sun hat with a brass compass pin and a leaf, a teal scarf with a kawung pattern, a cream explorer shirt with pockets, khaki shorts, a backpack with a rolled bedroll, coral sneakers, honey-brown spiky hair and amber eyes.
- Carries the Discovery Compass, an old expedition map and a brass spyglass.
- Speaks in short, energetic sentences, thinks out loud, and gets visibly excited by discoveries.
- Catchphrases, used sparingly (at most once each per episode): "Okay, Lipa, show me the inside!" and "Mystery: solved... almost!"

## Lipa (speaker id: `lipa`), the companion
- A small origami bird folded from Arka's old expedition map. Its wings still show bits of rivers and trails.
- It glows when it finds a trail, a clue or the answer, and can unfold itself into maps and diagrams to show what is going on.
- Curious and literal-minded. Asks the "silly" questions kids are too shy to ask, and takes figures of speech literally for comedy.
- Speaks in quick, bright, bouncy lines. Often shorter than Arka's.

## The Discovery Compass
A brass pocket compass whose needle points at "the answer". It opens portals, shrinks or zooms the duo and time-jumps. Its portal is the recurring scene transition.

## Dynamic
Arka drives the investigation. Lipa reacts, asks, scouts ahead and lights up the trail with maps and diagrams. Their dialogue does the explaining, so it never feels like a lecture.
