# Kids safety policy (ages 8–12, YouTube "Made for Kids")

Content must be:
- **Safe to imitate.** No dangerous stunts, fire, chemicals, electricity, heights, or looking at the Sun. A home experiment needs simple, safe materials and an adult-supervision line.
- **Not frightening.** Predators, storms, space dangers and the human body are shown with wonder, not fear or gore.
- **Accurate.** No misleading health, science or history claims. Kid-level simplifications must stay true.
- **Respectful.** No stereotypes, mocking, body-shaming, or violence played for laughs.
- **Privacy-safe.** Never ask kids for names, ages, locations, photos, or to contact anyone.
- **Not manipulative.** No pressure tactics, fake urgency, or "only smart kids watch to the end".
- **Brand-free.** No real brands, products, or copyrighted characters.
