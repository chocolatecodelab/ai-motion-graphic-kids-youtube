# Characters (v0: concept C "Kai & Needle", pre-Character Bible)

## Kai (speaker id: `kai`), the hero
- An inventive 10-year-old who *built* the Discovery Compass from parts found in a grandparent's attic.
- A maker: takes things apart to see how they work, gets things wrong and fixes them.
- Curious and brave, playful, humble. Kai doesn't know the answer and finds it out with the viewer.
- Looks: short hair, oversized teal hoodie with rolled sleeves, a utility belt with tools, orange sneakers, a band-aid on one knee.
- Speaks in short, energetic sentences, thinks out loud, makes guesses (sometimes wrong), and gets visibly excited by discoveries.
- Catchphrases, used sparingly (at most once each per episode): "Okay, Needle, show me the inside!" and "Mystery: solved... almost!"

## Needle (speaker id: `needle`), the companion
- A tiny holographic spark that lives inside the Discovery Compass: the compass's accidental AI.
- Curious and literal-minded. Asks the "silly" questions kids are too shy to ask, and takes figures of speech literally for comedy.
- Can project holograms and diagrams, and operates the compass's powers.
- Speaks in quick, bright, bouncy lines. Often shorter than Kai's.

## The Discovery Compass
A brass-and-teal pocket device whose needle points at "the answer". It opens portals, shrinks or zooms the duo, projects holograms and time-jumps. Its portal is the recurring scene transition.

## Dynamic
Kai drives the investigation. Needle reacts, asks, and projects visuals. Their dialogue does the explaining, so it never feels like a lecture.
